Development Journey

What you'll find here: The practical path used to build and validate the chatbot without relying on access to a company's Microsoft Teams environment.

Starting without company Teams access

The project can be developed locally first. The chatbot's matching engine, support topics, scoring, conversation flow, clarification behavior, and unresolved handling can be exercised through Microsoft 365 Agents Playground.

Why this matters

Lack of enterprise access does not have to block development of the core chatbot. Teams deployment becomes a later integration stage rather than a prerequisite for learning, coding, or validating the MVP.
